Winter's Nostalgia

The December where the breeze are cold, and the leaves are dry.
The very end of a year—that's for the year but it was my beginning.
New things to learn and a new life ahead.
It was a city to explore, soothing for that weather.
So I did that, not with the city but with myself.
Found myself—a new self which had been hidden for long.

❄️

A very ordinary day—or maybe the most suitable evening—to ask for my college.
With a pleasant smile, which I'll never forget for life.
My fortune—or maybe my curse—to ping her again.
The days we talked and the nights we walked stay as an illusion.
The way she walked beside me wearing a pink sweater with excitement.
Along with fear combined with joy, sharing things of life and past.
Those nights, the cold streets, the talks, the laughs and even the hot lamps.
Every moment stays in my memory, relentlessly asking to relive.

🌙

She carried bravery within a fragile heart and empathetic with care and innocent by face.
Befriended to a person who struggled with anger within himself.
Those walks made us forget the night, the time, but not the people around the streets.
Still it was hilarious to handle those situations.
But somehow those endless nights slowly faded away,
leaving only memories of conversations vanishing into silence.

☕

Slowly, the warmth of those live conversations faded into long midnight calls,
yet you still held the thread between us with quiet effort and care.
Most of the time it was war and storm,
sometimes it was a pleasant gentle breeze.

🩷

The day we went for shopping for that saree wasn't special because of the place.
It became unforgettable because you were beside me.

✨

During those FA1 days and location days,
I wished for your happiness more than my own.

🌸

Mysore will remain my favourite place on earth—
not because of the aesthetic spots,
but because of you.

— Saravanan

My Apology Poem

I don't have any prestige to lose, Sandhiya.
I don't have any ego that I need to protect.

But I do have one greed—
I'm greedy to be the closest person to you.

🌙

Maybe that's why sometimes I overthink.
Why a little distance feels like a thousand miles.
And why your silence can feel louder than any storm.

I don't want to win any argument with you.
I don't want my pride to stand between us.

✨

Because what's the point of being right,
if the person I want beside me feels far away?

The moon doesn't compete with stars.
The ocean doesn't fight with the shore.
They simply belong to the same beautiful universe,
each in their own way.

And maybe I just want to belong beside you like that.

💗

So if my words hurt you,
If my actions made you feel anything less than love...

I'm truly sorry.

Forgive my heart for being a little too greedy.
It only ever wanted one thing—

To be the person closest to your heart.

🌸
Shall we start again,
peacefully?
— Saravanan
